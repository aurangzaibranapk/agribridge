# Al Rana Traders — AgriBridge Mobile Privacy Policy (Draft)

**Effective date:** To be set before public launch

Al Rana Traders uses the AgriBridge mobile application to provide product ordering, farmer services, milk and grain records, machinery booking, dealer statements, staff work and account services.

## Data we process

Depending on the user's role and chosen service, the app may process name, mobile number, account role, branch/shop assignment, orders, payments, khata and wallet records, milk and grain records, machinery bookings, approximate or precise location when explicitly required, device notification token, and images voluntarily submitted for agricultural assistance.

## Why we process it

We use this information to authenticate users, deliver requested services, show the correct account records, enforce branch/shop permissions, prevent fraud, send operational notifications and provide customer support.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information is shared only with authorized Al Rana Traders personnel and service providers necessary to operate authentication, database, notifications, maps and AI-assisted services, subject to access controls.

## Security

The app uses authenticated sessions, database Row Level Security, role-based permissions, encrypted network transport and audit logging. No database service-role credential is stored in the mobile application.

## Retention and deletion

Records are retained only as required for service delivery, accounting, audit and legal obligations. Users may request account deletion from the app's Privacy & Security section or through the support contact published on the official website. Transaction records that must legally be retained may be anonymized or restricted rather than erased.

## Children

The app is intended for business customers, farmers, dealers and authorized staff, and is not designed for children.
